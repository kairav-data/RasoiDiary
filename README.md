# 🍛 Rasoi Diary | Her Indian Culinary Sanctuary

> **"स्वाद और सुगंध — Dedicated with love to her kitchen magic."**

An elegant, feature-rich web application custom-tailored for your wife to organize, create, and celebrate her treasured Indian food recipes.

---

## ✨ Key Features Specially Crafted for Her

### 1. 🪔 Personalized Chef Identity & Warm Aesthetics
- **Customizable Rasoi Branding**: She can personalize her name, kitchen title (e.g., *"Pooja's Rasoi"*), chef emoji avatar, and bio.
- **Warm Indian Culinary Theme**: Warm saffron, turmeric amber, cardamom green, and terracotta palette with elegant Indian typography.

### 2. 🌶️ Authentic Indian Cuisine Categorization
- **Regional Filters**: North Indian (Punjabi, Awadhi), South Indian (Chettinad, Kerala), West Indian (Gujarati, Marathi), East Indian, and Street Food.
- **Dietary Tags**: Pure Veg (*Shakahari*), Jain-friendly, Non-Veg, and Vegan.
- **Mirchi Heat Meter**: Mild 🌿, Medium 🌶️, Spicy 🌶️🌶️, and Desi Teekha 🔥.
- **Course Types**: Main Course, Breakfast (*Nashta*), Biryani & Rice, Chaat, Mithai & Desserts, Chai & Beverages.

### 3. 🧑‍🍳 Interactive Kitchen Cooking Mode (Hands-Free Friendly)
- Designed for tablet, phone, or laptop propped on the kitchen counter while cooking.
- **Distraction-Free Big Screen**: Large typography with high contrast.
- **Built-in Step Timers**: Automatic countdowns for simmering, pressure cooking whistles, and bhunao with temple chime alerts.
- **Voice Narration (Read Aloud 🔊)**: Text-to-Speech audio reader so she doesn't have to touch the screen with turmeric- or flour-dusted hands!
- **Celebration Confetti**: Festive celebration with sound when completing a recipe.

### 4. ⚖️ Dynamic Servings & Ingredient Scaler
- Seamlessly scale any recipe from 2 to 4, 6, 8, or custom servings with `+` / `-` buttons.
- Ingredients automatically recalculate with clean culinary fractions (e.g. 1/2 tsp, 1 1/2 cups, 250 g).

### 5. 🧂 Interactive Masala Dabba (Indian Spice Box & Substitution Guide)
- Traditional 7-spice box explorer: Haldi, Jeera, Rai, Dhania, Kashmiri Lal Mirch, Garam Masala, Hing, Kasuri Methi, and Elaichi.
- Explains flavor profiles, **exact Tadka (tempering) timing** in hot ghee/oil, and emergency substitutes if she runs out of an ingredient mid-cook!

### 6. 📅 "आज क्या बनेगा?" (Weekly Meal Planner)
- Monday-to-Sunday interactive planner for Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner.
- **"Surprise Me / Auto-Suggest Week"** button to eliminate daily meal planning fatigue.
- One-click **"Add Week to Grocery List"** button.

### 7. 🛒 Bazaar Grocery Shopping List & WhatsApp Share
- Ingredients organized by Indian bazaar sections: Fresh Produce (*Sabzi Mandi*), Dairy & Paneer, Spices & Masala Bhandar, Grains & Dals, and Pantry.
- **Send on WhatsApp Button 📱**: Format and send the shopping list straight to family/husband via WhatsApp with a single tap.

### 8. 💡 Dadi Maa's Secret Tips (*दादी माँ का नुस्खा*)
- Dedicated spotlight for generational cooking wisdom, tempering secrets, and restaurant-style gravy techniques on each recipe card.

### 9. 💾 Safe Backup & Offline Storage
- Recipes, meal plans, and grocery items persist automatically in local storage.
- Downloadable JSON backup anytime with the **Export Backup** button.

## 🚀 Running the Web App

The app is running locally:

```bash
# Start development server
npm run dev
```

Open your browser at: **[http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/)**

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Framework**: React 19 + Vite 8
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Audio & Speech**: Web Audio API (soothing chimes) & Web Speech API (hands-free kitchen narration)
- **Effects**: Canvas Confetti
